Privacy Policy

Last updated 10 October 2026

This policy explains what information Hard Truth Gospel Mission receives when you visit this website or order The House by Austine Ojo, and how that information is used. We aim to handle it in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

Visiting this website

You can read every part of this website without giving us any personal information. There are no forms, accounts or sign-ups, and we do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

  • Your appearance setting. If you choose Day or Night, that choice is saved in your own browser so the page looks the same next time. It never leaves your device, and you can clear it by clearing your browser's site data.
  • Hosting. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may record technical details such as your IP address when the page loads, to deliver and protect the service.
  • Fonts. The typefaces load from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address when the page loads.

Ordering the book

Orders are made by bank transfer and confirmed on WhatsApp. When you order, we receive what you choose to send us: your name, your WhatsApp number, your messages and your proof of payment. For a paperback, we also receive the delivery details you give us.

We use this information only to confirm your payment, deliver your book, answer your questions and keep a record of the sale. We do not sell it, and we do not share it with anyone except where we must to deliver a paperback or where the law requires it.

Your bank transfer is processed by your bank and ours (GTBank (Guaranty Trust Bank)) under their own terms. WhatsApp and Facebook are run by Meta under their own privacy policies.

Sharing this page

The share buttons on this site are plain links. Nothing is sent to WhatsApp, Facebook, X, Telegram or your email app until you press one, and then that service's own privacy policy applies.

How long we keep it

We keep order messages and payment confirmations for as long as we need them to support your order and keep proper records, and then delete them.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it where we no longer need it. Message us on WhatsApp at +234 803 700 8560 (opens in a new tab).

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle information, we will update this page and the date at the top.
